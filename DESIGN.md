## Brand & Style

This design system embodies quiet confidence, understated opulence, and architectural precision tailored for high-net-worth individuals, NRIs, and astute real estate investors managing assets in Hyderabad. The emotional signature is calm reassurance and unwavering trust—a feeling of institutional stewardship rather than transactional oversight. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Editorial Minimalism with Architectural Sophistication**. It purposefully eschews flashy, tech-heavy dashboard tropes, SaaS widgets, generic gradients, and artificial 3D fluff. Instead, the interface draws deep inspiration from premium global architectural monographs and high-end print publications: generous ivory margins, razor-sharp typographic hierarchy, restrained borders, and authentic, muted architectural imagery. 

Every layout prioritizes breathing room, high-contrast legibility, and refined composition. Vibrant brand accents are wielded deliberately as surgical focal points rather than decorative wallpaper, ensuring the platform conveys the gravitas of a multi-crore asset management partner.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic foundations with an iconic, razor-sharp accent:

- **Ivory (`#FFF9F3`) & Vanilla (`#F5E8D0`)**: The ambient heartbeat of the system. Off-white, warm, and natural, they form the primary viewport canvas and secondary structural zones, eliminating harsh digital glare.
- **Charcoal (`#252225`)**: The definitive ink. Used for headlines, high-contrast body typography, sharp linework, and dark-foundation utility bands (such as the footer). Never use pure black (`#000000`).
- **Primary Accent (`#F70252`)**: Reserved strictly for high-intent interactive gestures—primary action buttons, active navigation markers, vital verification tags, and the brand mark itself. It must never occupy large surface fills or wide background containers.
- **Support Tones (`#BF2B55`, `#A96B78`, `#F6D6DC`, `#761A38`)**: Provide contextual gradation. Use Soft Rose for non-interactive micro-pills or background chips; Deep Wine and Raspberry for rich editorial subheaders or hovered interactive boundaries.

Maintain at least an 85:10:5 distribution ratio across surfaces: 85% neutrals (Ivory, Vanilla, subtle dividers), 10% Charcoal ink/typography, and 5% primary chromatic accents.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial grace of **Newsreader** (a contemporary, high-elegance serif) with the architectural clarity and structural precision of **Manrope**.

- **Display & Headlines (Newsreader)**: Evokes the gravitas of architectural catalogues, heritage broadsheets, and luxury publications. Display headings prioritize optical balance with tight letter-spacing and organic line wraps. Keep copy concise—never stack multi-sentence narrative blocks into display treatments.
- **Body & Interface (Manrope)**: Delivers rapid scannability, neutral utilitarian poise, and high legibility across dense data (e.g., plot sizes, cadastral references, and legal specifications).
- **Label Caps**: Used for eyebrow categories, section tags (e.g., `KONU MYSECURE`), and status signals. Set exclusively in uppercase with wide letter tracking (`0.12em`).
- **Pacing**: Headings must breathe. Allow substantial margin bottoms before subsequent body lines. Body copy should be capped at 64 characters per line to maintain relaxed reading velocity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column architectural grid with strict horizontal and vertical cadence based on a 4px baseline (`0.25rem`).

### Grid Rules
- **Desktop (≥1200px)**: 12 columns, `container-max` of 1280px (`80rem`), 40px gutters, and 64px horizontal margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 24px gutters, and 32px horizontal margins.
- **Mobile (≤767px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, and 20px horizontal margins.

### Section Cadence & Rhythms
Sections rely on generous vertical intervals. Standard page sections mandate `space-24` (96px) to `space-32` (128px) of vertical padding on desktop, scaling down gracefully to `space-16` (64px) on mobile. Never crowd sections with artificial dividing boxes; let vertical whitespace and fine hairlines (`border-subtle`) delineate structure.

### Hero & Asymmetry
The hero viewport uses an asymmetric desktop distribution: a 5-column editorial text block on the left with a dominant 7-column photographic panel on the right. Imagery must maintain crisp aspect ratios (typically 4:5 or 16:10) and extend directly into baseline grids without soft, curved boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy drop shadows, synthetic neon glows, and aggressive glassmorphism. Depth is expressed tactically through **tonal layering and razor-thin hairlines**, echoing pristine physical print surfaces:

1. **Ground Canvas**: `#FFF9F3` (Ivory) acts as the base plane.
2. **Elevated Surfaces**: Secondary containers (pricing tiers, inquiry panels) rest directly on the base plane or transition to `#F5E8D0` (Vanilla) with an ultra-fine border: `1px solid rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.08)`.
3. **Ambient Floor Lift (Interactive Hover Only)**:
   - Rest State: `0 0 0 1px rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.08)`
   - Hover / Active State: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.06), 0 0 0 1px rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.12)`
   This produces a subtle photographic lift without smudging or dirtying the surrounding whitespace.
4. **Modals & Overlays**: Rest on an ambient scrim of `#252225` with 40% opacity, utilizing a restrained shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft, architectural geometry** (`roundedness: 1`). Elements feel meticulously cut and tailor-made rather than hyper-rounded or bubbly:

- **Buttons & Small Interactive Chips**: Rounded to `4px` (`0.25rem`). This preserves crisp, authoritative corners while taking the harsh sting off standard rectangular shapes.
- **Cards, Pricing Blocks, Modals**: Standardized at `8px` (`0.5rem`). Avoid larger radii (`16px+`), which dilute the serious architectural character of the brand.
- **Pills / Status Dots**: Micro tags (such as property verification indicators) may use full pill radiuses only when font-size is strictly `label-caps` (`12px` or below) and interior padding is compact.
- **Architectural Image Frames**: Sharp or micro-radius (`4px`). Never use large organic blobs, floating rounded cards over photography, or heavy circular crop masks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#F70252` fill, text in `#FFFFFF`, font `label-ui` (semi-bold), height 48px, horizontal padding `space-6` (24px), border-radius 4px. On hover: darken background to `#BF2B55` with a smooth 150ms transition.
- **Secondary Action**: Background transparent, border `1px solid #252225`, text `#252225`. On hover: background becomes `#252225` with text transitioning to `#FFF9F3`.
- **Tertiary / Link**: Plain text `#252225` with a static 1px underline in `rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.3)`. On hover: underline color shifts to `#F70252`.

### Cards & Content Modules
- Render cards on `#FFF9F3` or `#F5E8D0` with a clean `1px solid rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.08)` perimeter.
- Avoid stacking internal cards inside external cards. Keep content planar, using generous padding (`space-8` or 32px) and editorial typography hierarchy rather than segmented sub-boxes.

### Pricing Cards & Period Toggles
- **Toggle**: Segmented control resting on a `#F5E8D0` pill track. The active selection uses a crisp `#252225` background with `#FFF9F3` text.
- **Tier Cards**: Display numerical value with `numeric-price` (Newsreader 44px). The featured tier receives an architectural highlight: an accent line `2px solid #F70252` along the top edge or an ultra-subtle badge tagged `RECOMMENDED` in `label-caps`.
- **List Items**: Icon checks rendered in `#F70252`, followed by concise `body-sm` copy in `#252225`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height: 52px for single lines; text area minimum height: 120px.
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#FFF9F3`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.16)`, border-radius 4px.
- Typography: `body-md` in `#252225`. Placeholder set to `rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.4)`.
- Focus State: Border color switches cleanly to `#F70252` with no loud glow rings—only a crisp `1px` outline ring: `0 0 0 1px #F70252`.

### Trust Strip & Feature Bars
- A horizontal structural band with minimal top and bottom borders (`1px solid rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.08)`).
- Items arranged in 4 symmetrical columns on desktop, 2x2 on mobile. Each entry features an ultra-fine, single-color line icon or numeric index `01-04` followed by crisp, punchy labels in `label-ui`.

### Accordion (FAQ)
- Dividers: Single horizontal line `1px solid rgba(37, 34, 37, 0.12)` separating entries.
- Question: `title-editorial` or `headline-sm`, padded vertically by `space-6` (24px).
- Indicator: Minimal `+` / `−` glyph in Charcoal (`#252225`), transitioning to `#F70252` upon expansion.
- Answer: `body-md` in Charcoal with an airy `line-height` of 1.65.
