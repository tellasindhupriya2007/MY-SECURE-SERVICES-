QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions About Property Monitoring in Hyderabad

1. What is Konu MySecure?expand_more
Konu MySecure is a dedicated property monitoring service based in Hyderabad. We conduct regular site visits, take verified photo updates, check fences and boundary stones, and keep owners clearly informed about their properties.
2. How often will my property be visited?expand_more
All standard MySecure plans include bi-weekly physical visits (twice a month) conducted by our full-time verified field team with location-tagged photos.
3. Can NRIs use Konu MySecure?expand_more
Yes. A large portion of our patrons are NRIs residing across North America, the Middle East, Europe, and Australasia who require trusted, institutional eyes on their Hyderabad properties without depending on casual favors from relatives.
4. What types of properties can MySecure monitor?expand_more
We monitor open residential plots, layout parcels, farmlands, independent villas, gated community residences, locked apartments, and commercial asset envelopes.
5. Can Konu monitor vacant plots in Hyderabad?expand_more
Yes. Vacant layout plots and acreage holdings are the most vulnerable to boundary stone tampering, debris dumping, and clandestine occupancy. Our bi-weekly visits are specifically optimized to safeguard vacant plots.
6. How much does property monitoring in Hyderabad cost?expand_more
Subscriptions start at ₹9,999 + GST for 6 months (for plots below 1,000 Sq. Yds.) and ₹14,999 + GST for an annual plan, with transparent pricing scaling by property acreage.
7. Will I receive photos of my property?expand_more
Yes. After every visit, our team takes multiple clear, high-resolution photos showing boundary markers, gates, street view, and the entire plot, which are uploaded to your Konu dashboard.
8. Does MySecure help prevent encroachment?expand_more
Consistent visits combined with visible Konu monitoring boards help deter trespassers and dumpers. Catching unauthorized activity early lets you take immediate action before problems grow.
9. Does MySecure include legal verification?expand_more
MySecure subscriptions cover visual on-ground monitoring and area alerts. Comprehensive legal verification, title title-flow documentation, and encumbrance audits are available as integrated add-on services from Konu’s property legal desk.
10. Does MySecure include property valuation?expand_more
While periodic macro area development alerts are part of your subscription, certified RICS bank-grade asset valuation can be commissioned separately via Konu’s valuation panel.
11. Can I monitor more than one property?expand_more
Yes. Multiple properties across different locations in Hyderabad can be linked under a single owner dashboard with custom reporting schedules and multi-asset consolidation.
12. What areas of Hyderabad does MySecure cover?expand_more
We cover Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) and HMDA growth belts including Gachibowli, Kokapet, Financial District, Shankarpally, Mokila, Tellapur, Shamshabad, Kompally, Medchal, Ghatkesar, Adibatla, and surrounding ORR/RRR micro-markets.
